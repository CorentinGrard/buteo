A full-screen flight screen with up to twelve tiles, a shorter menu,
and a page for each recorded flight.

> **A backup instrument.** Buteo is no certified instrument: never
> use it as your only source for navigation, airspace or traffic.
> It is not made by the XCSoar project; report its problems in this
> repository's issues.

## What is new

- The flight screen is full screen: the phone's status and gesture
  bars hide, and a swipe from an edge shows them for a few seconds
- Two, three or four rows of three tiles (Quick settings → Rows). A
  layout saved with six tiles gets two more rows, the last with flight
  time, local time and battery, which the status bar no longer shows
- The Menu button is the first of the map buttons, at the bottom
  left, and no longer cuts the target card short
- A shorter menu, 11 rows instead of 17. Pages that belong together
  open from one another: the pilot and WeGlide from Aircraft & crew,
  NOTAMs from Airspace, units from Map & units, the wind from Flight
  setup. The vario sound and the voice callouts are one Sound page;
  Status is the last row
- Each flight in Flights opens its page: times, contest result, climbs
  and highest point, read from its IGC file, with Replay, Share,
  Upload to WeGlide and Delete (Replay and Delete on the ground). The
  Replay page and the demo flight are gone: replay a flight from its
  page
- No "Airspace alerts off" chip on the map; the menu's Airspace row
  says whether warnings are on

## Fixed

- The APK installed on 32-bit and x86 phones, where Buteo cannot
  start; Android now refuses it there

## Install

Android 8 or newer, on a 64-bit ARM phone. It updates 1.0.0 in place
and keeps your files.

- With [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), which
  keeps Buteo up to date: see the
  [README](https://github.com/CorentinGrard/buteo#readme)
- By hand: download `buteo-1.1.0.apk` below on the phone, open it, and
  let the browser install apps when Android asks
