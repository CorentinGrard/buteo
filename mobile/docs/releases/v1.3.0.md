A new map that works without a network, a pre-flight check of your
data, routes you switch in one tap, and a circling view that holds
the thermal.

> **A backup instrument.** Buteo is no certified instrument: never
> use it as your only source for navigation, airspace or traffic.
> It is not made by the XCSoar project; report its problems in this
> repository's issues.

## What is new

- One map, drawn by MapLibre, replaces XCSoar's: roads, towns, water
  and forests from OpenStreetMap, the relief from your terrain file,
  your airspace, waypoints and route on top. It needs no network in
  flight: its data comes in region files, one per region of the world,
  updated every month
- Pre-flight check (Menu → Pre-flight check): whether the terrain, the
  map, the airspace and the waypoints cover your route (or 50 km
  around you), and how recent each file is. On Wi-Fi and on the
  ground, Buteo downloads the map regions your route crosses and the
  newer versions of your files by itself; "Download all" does it on a
  mobile network
- The task is now called the route (French keeps "Circuit"). While
  you fly one, the Route page shows it alone: Previous, Next, Restart,
  Edit and Stop. Without one, your saved routes: a tap shows it on the
  map, "Fly this route" flies it. Every route you fly is saved by its
  name
- Go to pauses the route; the Route page offers to resume it.
  "Finish at" the start, for the usual flight back home
- Circling, the map holds the thermal and the glider circles round
  it, as on XCSoar's map. A sky blue dot marks the thermal's core,
  with the thermal assistant on it; earlier thermals show as dots
  with their average climb
- Trail drift (Wind page) now works: while circling, the trail drifts
  with the wind so that the circles stack over the thermal
- A clearer menu: the in-flight buttons in a band at the bottom,
  under your thumb, and an icon for each entry. Flight setup shows
  its MacCready, bugs, QNH and ballast
- Flights are listed and sorted by the date of the flight in the IGC
  file, not the date the file was copied
- A replay no longer raises airspace alerts: no banner, sound,
  vibration or voice, and no airspace in red on the map
- The team mate's code takes three to five letters or digits, as in
  XCSoar

## Faster

- Buteo's own code is compiled ahead of time on your phone, and the
  flight screen does less work every second: less CPU, especially
  while circling

## Fixed

- A replay's last airspace warning stayed on the screen after the
  replay stopped, and "Day" pressed during a replay silenced that
  airspace for the rest of the day's real flight
- Reach and arrival heights over terrain used a 16 times coarser
  terrain while Buteo was in the background
- Before the first GPS fix, the target card read "0.00 m" and 000°
- The final glide bar cut the last digit of a large number, and a
  short tile title was cut beside its number ("ALT B…")
- SkyLines live tracking now keeps one position every 25 seconds
  while the phone has no network, as intended (an XCSoar fix)

## Install

Android 8 or newer, on a 64-bit ARM phone. It updates 1.2.0, 1.1.0
and 1.0.0 in place and keeps your files. After the update, open
Menu → Pre-flight check on Wi-Fi to download the map of your region.

- With [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), which
  keeps Buteo up to date: see the
  [README](https://github.com/CorentinGrard/buteo#readme)
- By hand: download `buteo-1.3.0.apk` below on the phone, open it, and
  let the browser install apps when Android asks
