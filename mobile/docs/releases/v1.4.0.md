Your own glide cone, a page for every airfield with its approach
chart and weather, the best field in reach always on top, and a replay
you scrub like a video.

> **A backup instrument.** Buteo is no certified instrument: never
> use it as your only source for navigation, airspace or traffic.
> It is not made by the XCSoar project; report its problems in this
> repository's issues.

## What is new

- Safety glide ratio (Menu → Safety heights): your glide cone, off or
  10 to 40. A field counts as in reach only at that glide ratio, with
  the wind and the terrain, arriving at your arrival height; the
  safety MacCready is 0 meanwhile. Reach, the fields in reach and the
  alternates follow it; final glide and speed to fly keep your
  glider's polar. When no field is left in the cone: a tone, a
  vibration and "Out of the glide cone, no field in reach"
- The map draws your glide range again: a dashed green line, where you
  can glide above the terrain clearance
- Tap an airfield on the map for its page: Go to, elevation, runway,
  frequency (to your radio), distance and reach, the waypoint file's
  notes, its METAR and TAF, and for French fields SIA's approach chart
  (VAC) of the current cycle, shown in Buteo and kept on the phone for
  flights without network
- Pre-flight check: the approach charts of the French airports along
  your route, downloaded by themselves on Wi-Fi
- The best field in reach (XCSoar's first alternate) beside the next
  point, in your glide cone: its distance and the height left above
  the arrival height. Three ways to try in Menu → Quick settings →
  Field (Rows, Pages, Chip); tell us which one to keep
- Circling: the climb around the thermal as a colour scale of past
  lift on the map, to find the core
- Replay: a slider over the flight from take-off to landing, like a
  video's; let go, and the replay jumps there
- A replay says nothing: no callouts, tones or vibrations

## Faster

- Pages and menus open at once: they waited for the map's next frame

## Fixed

- "No landable in reach" warned once a flight at most: it now warns
  again once a field has been back in reach for a minute (an XCSoar
  fix)

## Install

Android 8 or newer, on a 64-bit ARM phone. It updates 1.3.0 and
earlier versions in place and keeps your files.

- With [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), which
  keeps Buteo up to date: see the
  [README](https://github.com/CorentinGrard/buteo#readme)
- By hand: download `buteo-1.4.0.apk` below on the phone, open it, and
  let the browser install apps when Android asks
